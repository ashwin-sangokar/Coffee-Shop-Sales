# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the sales performance of a coffee shop using **Microsoft Excel**.  
The objective was to transform raw transactional data into **actionable business insights** through data cleaning, analysis, and an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly understand:
- Sales trends
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product performance
- Store-wise performance

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)
  - Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
  - Slicers (Month, Day filters)
  - Calculated fields & KPIs
- Data visualization best practices

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
- **Total Sales**
- **Total Footfall**
- **Average Bill per Person**
- **Average Orders per Person**

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed
- **Hourly Sales Analysis**  
  Identifies peak business hours and demand patterns.
  
- **Category-wise Sales Distribution**  
  Shows revenue contribution of each product category.
  
- **Store Location Performance**  
  Compares footfall and revenue across different store locations.
  
- **Product Size Distribution**  
  Analyzes customer preference based on order size.
  
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue**  
  Highlights best-selling products.
  
- **Weekly Order Trends**  
  Identifies the busiest days of the week.

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- Peak sales occur during **morning hours**, indicating strong breakfast demand.
- The **Coffee category dominates sales**, contributing the highest share of revenue.
- Some store locations generate **higher revenue with similar footfall**, suggesting better pricing or product mix.
- **Medium and Large sizes** account for the majority of orders.
- **Weekdays outperform weekends** in total order volume.
