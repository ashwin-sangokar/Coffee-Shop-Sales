☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard)
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance of a coffee shop using Microsoft Excel.
The goal was to convert raw transactional data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, analysis, and an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand:

Sales trends
Customer behavior
Product performance
Store-wise performance

🛠 Tools & Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel
  Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)
  Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
  Slicers (Month, Day filters)
  Calculated fields & KPIs
Data Visualization best practices

📊 Key KPIs

Total Sales
Total Footfall
Average Bill per Person
Average Orders per Person

📈 Analysis Performed

Hourly Sales Analysis – identifies peak business hours
Category-wise Sales Distribution – contribution of each product category
Store Location Performance – footfall vs revenue comparison
Product Size Distribution – customer preference by size
Top 5 Products by Revenue – identifies best-selling items
Weekly Order Trends – busiest days of the week

📌 Key Insights

Peak sales occur during morning hours, indicating strong breakfast demand
Coffee category dominates sales, contributing the highest revenue share
Certain store locations generate higher revenue with similar footfall, indicating better pricing or product mix
Medium and Large sizes account for the majority of orders
Weekdays outperform weekends in total orders

Structuring raw data for analysis
Designing KPI-driven dashboards
Translating data into business insights
